Xeno-Cooperation in Biology and Beyond Conference 2026

Homepage About Programme Abstracts Organisers

Conference Organised by the Cooperation and Evolutionary Ecology Research Group. It is a 1-day, small-scale symposium on the Evolution of cooperation between agents with different capabilities and characteristics, in Budapest, Hungary in autumn 2026.

The aim of this meeting is to enable scholars from different backgrounds to present their theories, methods, and research findings to create opportunities to foster interdisciplinary collaborations. The workshop will include approximately 10+ researchers from Europe, from various fields of Biology, both theoretical and experimental.

The meeting would be on the 17th of September (Thursday), starting 10:00 and ending around 17:00 at 1121 Budapest, Konkoly-Thege Miklós út 29-33. The venue can be easily reached with bus line 221.
