About the Symposium

Our research group's focus to study different aspects of cooperation and altruism on many different levels in highly variable cases, from microbial to Human level. We do both theoretical and experimental investigations for that.

The aim of this symposium is to bring those researchers together that are working on different aspects of social behaviour. The long term purpose is to make opportunity for networking and possibility for long term collaborations.

On the symposium there will be 11 participants. The topic of the talks ranges from EGYIK TÉMA to MÁSIK TÉMA.
